Steering the model — the multi-layer, multi-vector study

29 August 2026 · 3,034 configurations × prompts = 12,136 generated clips over 7 nodes. Every row is paired against the α = 0 baseline that ran on the same prompts in the same job: each prompt's 4 samples are averaged first, so n is the number of prompts. Rows shaded green are free — WER median ≤ 0.20 and Δ WER ≤ +0.06. Plan pre-registered before the runs in protocols/steering_study_plan.md.

The headline: the delivery-style axes steer far better than emotion, and several of them are free. Pushing S_DRAM on its top 3 layers moves the VoiceNet dramatic regression by +1.78 (t = 8.58, 6 of 6 prompts) while the word error rate improves (median 0.084, Δ −0.044). S_WHIS at one layer moves +1.85 with Δ WER −0.065. S_RANT reaches +3.95 (t = 8.75) at five layers. Emotion, at its best free setting, moves +0.10. That ordering tracks decodability exactly: W1 measured R² 0.83 for S_RANT against 0.47 for the emotion heads — you can steer what the model represents well, and emotion is the thing it represents badly.

More layers buys more emotion and pays for it superlinearly in intelligibility. k = 1 is the only free band: +0.067 to +0.122 with WER median 0.065–0.102 against a 0.056 baseline. At k = 3 the gain roughly doubles to +0.21 but the WER median is 0.38 — one clip in three is no longer the text that was asked for. Beyond k = 5 the gain collapses back while WER stays destroyed: k = 7 at α = 0.12 is +0.035 (t = 0.46) with WER median 0.991. There is a ridge, and it is narrow.
And the random control is not null at k = 3. A random direction on three layers moves emo_pct by +0.081 (t = 2.04) in job 2's slot set. Multi-layer emotion gains have to be read against that, not against zero — it eats roughly a third of what k = 3 appears to buy. At k = 1, k = 5 and k = 7 the random control is flat (+0.040, +0.008, −0.021).

Subtracting numbness is not an emotion amplifier — it is a genuineness preserver, and a remarkably clean one. At one layer, emotion +0.10 alone costs −0.86 of genuineness. The pre-registered prediction was right, and the trade is favourable. What this changes

  1. Ship style steering, not emotion steering. S_DRAM, S_WHIS, S_ASMR, S_CASU and S_NEWS move their own axis by a large margin at no measurable cost in intelligibility. Emotion at the same budget moves a tenth as far.
  2. k is per attribute, and it is small. Genuineness, blend and aesthetics break at k ≥ 2. Emotion is free only at k = 1. S_DRAM and S_RANT want 3 to 5. There is no global answer, which is exactly why the tap ranking had to be per dimension.
  3. Emotion steering should carry a numbness subtraction. −0.10 on Emotional_Numbness removes the entire genuineness cost of a +0.10 emotion push while keeping three quarters of the gain.
  4. Report the random control at every k. It is null at k = 1 and not null at k = 3. Any multi-layer claim without it is unreadable.

What is still open

  • 41 of the 57 VoiceNet dimensions — the timbre and resonance axes were deferred. The performance axes moved, so the case for running them is now made.
  • German. Everything here is English.
  • Total injected magnitude was never controlled. k layers at α is k times the perturbation of one layer at α, so the k ladder confounds "more layers" with "more push". The realised per-tap magnitudes are logged, so a matched-budget re-analysis is possible without new generation.
  • No listening test. Every number here is an automatic scorer, including the ones being steered — a model steering itself towards another model's judgement.
